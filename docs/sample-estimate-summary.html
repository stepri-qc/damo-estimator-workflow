Assumptions

  • A1 Coverage is 24×5: 24 hours a day, 5 days a week, split into 3 shifts of 8 hours. Shift handover overlap is absorbed inside the shift. [F] 1.1 C
  • A2 Each FTE works 40 contracted hours a week, so covers 5.00 shifts. [F] p.3 b
  • A3 Each resource is productive 85% of contracted hours (training, meetings, admin), so the roster is divided by 85%. Leave and holidays are handled separately. [F] G6
  • A4 At least 1 L2 and 1 L3 on every shift, whatever the ticket volume. Ticket-driven effort is sized separately from Stage 2 and compared with this team. [F] p.3 c
  • A5 Shifts are covered from India (2), Romania (1). Each location keeps its own roster. [F] G3
  • A6 Leave and public holidays are backfilled on a 260-day working year per location. proposed
  • A7 The tool sizes FTE only. Rates, cost and price are out of scope. agreed
  • U1 Service desk (L1) is provided by the client and is out of scope for this team.
