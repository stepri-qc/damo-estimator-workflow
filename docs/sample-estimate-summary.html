Assumptions register

Coverage & locations
  • Step 1 Coverage is 24×5: 24 hours a day from 00:00 customer time, 5 days a week, worked in 3 location windows. Each window is staffed for its full length, and overlap between locations is staffed twice. 80 hours a week are active and 40 are on-call for P1 incidents only, counted at 15.0% of staffed time. [F] 1.1 C
  • Step 1 At least 1 L2 and 1 L3 on duty in every staffed hour, whatever the ticket volume. Ticket-driven effort is sized separately from Step 2 and compared with this team. [F] p.3 c
  • Step 1 Windows are worked from India (2), Romania (1), each in its own time zone (India UTC+5:30, Romania UTC+2) against a customer clock of UTC+0. Each location keeps its own roster. [F] G3
  • Step 1 Leave and public holidays are backfilled on a 260-day working year per location. proposed
  • Step 2 Tower FTE gets the same leave and holiday backfill as the base team (×1.123, weighted by location), so the two teams are compared like for like at Step 3. [F] G3
  • Step 5 Specialists sit in the locations covering their shifts, with the same leave and holiday backfill as Step 1. [F] G3
Demand & volumes
  • Step 2 The client runs the L1 service desk. Unless a tower says otherwise, tickets are L1 inflow: 70% pass to L2 and 20% of those to L3 (Year 1 benchmark). [F] 1.4
  • Step 2 Ticket history is used for AMS tower. No volumetrics for IMS tower: demand comes from structural complexity (T-shirt sizing for applications). [F] Sc.1 Steps 1–2
  • Step 2 IMS, DMS and AI platform proxies use complexity points × tickets per point (IMS 4.5, DMS 3.4, AI 3.5). These are calibration targets. proposed
Service scope & ownership
  • Step 1 The tool sizes FTE only. Rates, cost and price are out of scope. agreed
  • Step 1 Service desk (L1) is provided by the client and is out of scope for this team. deal team
  • Step 2 AMS tower: we provide L2 and L3 (1 interface with client, +4% coordination). IMS tower: we provide L2 and L3 (1 interface with client, +4% coordination). input
SLA & service levels
  • Step 2 SLA uplift on L2/L3 effort where targets are stringent: AMS tower (P1 response ≤30 min +12%, P1 restore ≤2 h +10% on L3). Capped at 40%. [F] Sc.3 Step 2
Effort benchmarks
  • Step 1 Each FTE works 40 contracted hours a week. [F] p.3 b
  • Step 1 Each resource is productive 85% of contracted hours (training, meetings, admin), so the roster is divided by 85%. Leave and holidays are handled separately. [F] G6
  • Step 2 Average resolution effort is 3h at L2 and 10h at L3, at 85% utilisation and 160 hours per FTE a month. [F] 1.5 · p.3
  • Step 2 Non-ticketing effort (automation, CFR, patching, governance) is 25% of A for a modern estate and 35% for complex legacy. [F] 1.1 B
Skills & specialists
  • Step 2 SRE is a named capability for IMS tower: a Service Reliability Engineer takes 78% of each of those towers' L3 FTE; the rest stays with their other skill sets. No extra headcount. proposed · damo-estimator role policy
  • Step 2 Skill sets the base team already has (AMS tower · Systems Support Engineer (L2/L3), AMS tower · Front end dev, IMS tower · Service Reliability Engineer (SRE), IMS tower · Infra support engineer) are covered on every shift by the base roster. Other skill sets get their own window cover in step 5. proposed
  • Step 2 Tower FTE keeps its effort-based L2/L3 split and is spread across locations like the Step 1 roster. Step 5 sets which windows each skill covers. proposed
  • Step 5 Skill sets the base team has are present on every shift through the Step 1 roster: Systems Support Engineer (L2/L3), Front end dev, Service Reliability Engineer (SRE), Infra support engineer. Step 1
  • Step 5 Other skill sets are live on the shifts picked here, with the stated people per shift. Outside them they are on call, counted as 15% of a staffed shift. proposed
  • Step 5 A skill set's FTE is the higher of its Step 2 effort and its shift coverage floor. This stage adds only the difference. proposed
Transformation & AIOps
  • Step 2 AIOps is in scope for AMS tower: one AI engineer per 4 support FTE of those towers (after Step 2 risk), counted as L3 engineering. They build intelligent alerting, RCA automation and self-healing, own prompt engineering and model evaluation, and do not staff shifts. [F] Sc.4 Step 3
  • Step 6 Scenario 3 (Low AI · volumetrics available). Volumetrics count as available when towers with ticket history carry at least half of the tower FTE. [F] Part 2–3
  • Step 6 Transformation POD is 12.5% of the support team from the work (steps 2 and 5, without AI engineers) (scenario guidance 10–15% (proposed)). It covers SRE, automation, CI/CD and AI adoption. [F] 1.7
  • Step 6 AI engineers (AIOps) are sized per tower in Step 2 at 1:4 and sit inside the Transformation POD, so the POD adds only what they don't already cover. [F] Sc.4 Step 3
  • Step 6 AIOps timeline for this scenario: Y2 onwards. This stage sizes steady-state capacity; the year-by-year ramp is set in Step 8. [F] Part 3
  • Step 6 Transformation FTE is engineering capacity (L3) and is spread across locations like the Step 1 roster. proposed
Governance & SDM
  • Step 3 One Service Delivery Manager per 8 delivery FTE, graded Lead. The SDM owns governance: ops reviews, QBRs, SLA reporting and CAB. DAMO convention · [F] 1.7
  • Step 3 The SDM is the only governance role. Delivery Principal, Solution Architect and similar roles are not included. agreed
  • Step 3 The SDM is based in Romania and is not rostered on windows. With whole-person rounding, SDMs round to the nearest person (at least one), not up. proposed
  • Step 3 The Part 1 delivery team is the larger of the base team (step 1, with risk) and the team from the towers (step 2, with risk). A lift follows the base team's locations and tiers. Part 2 re-bases the SDM on the final delivery team. agreed
Seniority & contingency
  • Step 7 Engagement complexity is Medium: mixed estate, several integrations, some regulated flows. Set by the deal team. input
  • Step 7 Grade pyramid Lead : Senior : Consultant = 1 : 2 : 3 across L2 and L3. SDMs are Lead. The pyramid does not change headcount. DAMO pyramid 1:2:4 · proposed
  • Step 7 Contingency is 7.5% of the delivery team plus SDM, added as FTE and spread across L2/L3 in each location. [F] 1.6 · 5–10%
  • Step 7 Grades describe the team shape only. No rates or cost are applied. agreed
Term & savings
  • Step 8 Year 1 is the Step 7 team. Savings start in Year 2; nothing is committed in Year 1. [F] Sc.2 · Sc.3 Step 5
  • Step 8 FTE falls 13.5% a year from Year 2 (Scenario 3 target 12–15%), from runbook maturity, lower pass-through and automation. [F] Part 3
  • Step 8 Savings do not reduce the Step 1 base team (6.1 FTE), which the support window needs. proposed
  • Step 8 Contingency is 7.5% in Year 1 and 6% from Year 2. [F] 1.6
  • Step 8 Scope and volumes stay at the baseline for the whole term. Growth beyond the volume band is re-baselined, not absorbed. [F] Part 3 volume band
