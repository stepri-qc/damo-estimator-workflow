Part 1 · Step 1 of 9

Base team from coverage windows and locations

The support window and the delivery locations' working hours set the smallest L2 + L3 team that can staff every hour. No ticket volumes are used. Step 2 builds a second team from the ticket volume. The two are compared at step 3: the larger one is used, so the base team is the floor.
